# Test Scenarios

50 sample project descriptions used to test the AI Project Charter Generator, grouped into 5 categories. Category 5 contains edge cases with missing, conflicting, or unrealistic inputs.


## Category 1: IT and software projects

**1. Employee Leave Management System**  
Develop a web-based leave management system for a company with 500 employees. Employees should submit leave requests, managers should approve or reject them, and HR should track leave balances. The system should send email notifications and maintain an approval history.

**2. Customer Relationship Management Implementation**  
Implement a CRM platform for a sales team of 40 employees to centralize customer information, track sales opportunities, automate follow-ups, and generate monthly sales reports. Complete implementation within four months.

**3. E-commerce Website Launch**  
Build and launch an e-commerce website for a fashion retailer selling 300 products. Include product search, shopping cart, secure checkout, order tracking, and an administrative dashboard. Target launch is in 16 weeks.

**4. Cloud Migration Project**  
Migrate 25 internal business applications from on-premises servers to a cloud environment. Minimize downtime, maintain data integrity, establish access controls, and train the IT support team before completion.

**5. Mobile Banking Application**  
Develop a mobile banking application that allows customers to view balances, transfer funds, download statements, and receive transaction alerts. The project must include security testing, user acceptance testing, and regulatory review.

**6. IT Help Desk Automation**  
Implement an IT service management platform to categorize support tickets, assign priorities, route requests, and track resolution times. The organization receives approximately 1,000 support tickets per month.

**7. University Learning Management System**  
Deploy a learning management system for a university with 5,000 students and 300 faculty members. Required features include course materials, assignments, grades, announcements, and integration with student records.

**8. Cybersecurity Awareness Program**  
Launch a cybersecurity awareness program for 800 employees. Include phishing simulations, online training, completion tracking, and a final assessment. Complete the initial rollout within 12 weeks.

**9. API Integration for Order Processing**  
Integrate the company's e-commerce platform with its inventory management and shipping systems. Ensure order details, stock availability, and shipment tracking information synchronize reliably.

**10. Business Intelligence Dashboard**  
Develop a business intelligence dashboard combining sales, finance, and operations data from four departments. Provide role-based access, standardized KPIs, and monthly performance reporting.

## Category 2: Project management and business operations

**11. Office Relocation Project**  
Relocate 150 employees from an existing office to a new facility within three months. Coordinate lease requirements, IT infrastructure, furniture procurement, employee communication, and business continuity while staying within a ₹40 lakh budget.

**12. New Product Launch**  
Launch a reusable water bottle product within six months. Coordinate product design, supplier selection, quality testing, packaging, marketing, distribution, and launch readiness. The target market and sales forecast are still being finalized.

**13. Employee Onboarding Process Improvement**  
Redesign the onboarding process for a company hiring 50 employees per quarter. Map existing workflows, identify delays, standardize documentation, assign responsibilities, and measure onboarding completion time.

**14. Corporate Event Management**  
Organize a two-day industry conference for 500 attendees within a ₹25 lakh budget. Manage the venue, speakers, registration, catering, audiovisual equipment, sponsorships, and emergency arrangements.

**15. Project Portfolio Management Office Setup**  
Establish a PMO for an organization running 20 concurrent projects. Define governance standards, reporting templates, risk escalation procedures, portfolio dashboards, and project review meetings.

**16. Business Process Automation**  
Automate repetitive administrative tasks across HR, finance, and procurement. Identify suitable workflows, evaluate automation tools, conduct pilot tests, train employees, and measure processing time and error rates.

**17. Vendor Management System Implementation**  
Introduce a centralized vendor management system for 100 suppliers. Include vendor onboarding, documentation, performance scorecards, contract tracking, compliance checks, and renewal reminders.

**18. Corporate Sustainability Initiative**  
Develop a 12-month sustainability program to reduce office energy consumption and paper usage. Establish baseline measurements, implement employee initiatives, track monthly results, and prepare quarterly progress reports.

**19. Customer Service Improvement Program**  
Improve customer support operations for a business handling 3,000 inquiries monthly. Assess current response times, introduce standardized workflows, train agents, and monitor customer satisfaction and resolution rates.

**20. Merger Integration Project**  
Integrate the HR, finance, IT, and operational processes of two recently merged companies over nine months. Identify dependencies, align policies, manage system integration, and communicate changes to employees. The combined budget has not yet been approved.

## Category 3: Supply chain, procurement, and manufacturing

**21. Inventory Optimization Initiative**  
Analyze inventory across 500 SKUs to identify excess stock, stockouts, slow-moving products, and replenishment issues. Establish ABC classification, inventory policies, and performance KPIs.

**22. Supplier Performance Improvement**  
Evaluate 30 suppliers based on delivery reliability, defect rates, pricing, and responsiveness. Create supplier scorecards and develop corrective action plans for underperforming vendors.

**23. Warehouse Management System Deployment**  
Implement a warehouse management system in a facility handling 10,000 order lines weekly. Configure inventory tracking, barcode scanning, picking workflows, dispatch processes, and employee training.

**24. Procurement Cost Reduction Project**  
Review annual procurement expenditure of ₹5 crore across five categories. Identify opportunities for supplier consolidation, competitive bidding, contract renegotiation, and purchasing standardization. No savings target has been approved yet.

**25. Demand Forecasting Improvement**  
Develop a demand forecasting process using 24 months of sales history across 200 products. Compare forecasting methods, evaluate accuracy against actual demand, and establish a monthly planning cycle.

**26. International Shipment Coordination**  
Coordinate an international shipment of industrial products from India to the UAE. Manage supplier readiness, export documentation, packaging, freight booking, customs coordination, and delivery confirmation.

**27. Manufacturing Quality Improvement**  
Reduce recurring defects on an assembly line producing 2,000 units per day. Establish defect baselines, perform root-cause analysis, introduce corrective actions, and track quality indicators over 12 weeks.

**28. Supplier Onboarding Automation**  
Digitize supplier registration for a procurement department working with 150 vendors. Standardize document collection, approval workflows, compliance verification, and vendor master-data creation.

**29. Cold Chain Logistics Improvement**  
Improve temperature-controlled distribution of perishable products across three regional warehouses. Define monitoring procedures, excursion alerts, handling standards, and escalation responsibilities.

**30. Procurement Digital Transformation**  
Transition a mid-sized company's purchasing operations from spreadsheets and email to a centralized procurement platform. Include requisitions, purchase orders, approval workflows, supplier records, and spend reporting.

## Category 4: Engineering, infrastructure, and sustainability

**31. Solar Rooftop Installation**  
Install a 250 kW rooftop solar system at a manufacturing facility. Manage feasibility assessment, structural review, vendor selection, permits, installation, grid connection, commissioning, and safety inspections.

**32. Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure**  
Install 20 EV charging stations across a corporate campus. Assess electrical capacity, select equipment, coordinate civil and electrical works, establish payment or access controls, and complete safety testing.

**33. Factory Equipment Modernization**  
Replace five aging production machines with modern equipment over six months. Coordinate procurement, site preparation, installation, operator training, acceptance testing, and production continuity.

**34. Smart Building Energy Management**  
Implement a building energy management system across a 100,000-square-foot commercial facility. Integrate available sensors and HVAC controls, establish energy baselines, and monitor system performance.

**35. Water Treatment Plant Upgrade**  
Upgrade a municipal water treatment facility to improve treatment capacity and monitoring. Coordinate engineering design, equipment procurement, construction, testing, regulatory approvals, and phased commissioning.

**36. Telecommunications Network Expansion**  
Expand broadband coverage to 10 underserved communities within nine months. Manage site surveys, equipment procurement, civil works, installation, connectivity testing, and local stakeholder coordination.

**37. Data Center Power Resilience**  
Improve power resilience at a small data center by reviewing backup power, UPS capacity, generator performance, and emergency procedures. Plan testing and upgrades while minimizing service interruptions.

**38. Industrial Safety Improvement**  
Implement a safety improvement program at a manufacturing plant with 250 workers. Conduct hazard assessments, update procedures, deliver training, assign corrective actions, and track incident and near-miss reporting.

**39. Public Transport Ticketing Modernization**  
Replace paper-based ticketing on a regional bus network with a digital ticketing system. Coordinate equipment deployment, payment integration, operator training, accessibility, and pilot testing.

**40. Electronic Waste Recycling Program**  
Establish an electronic waste collection and recycling program for a company operating across 15 offices. Define collection procedures, authorized recycling partnerships, data-security requirements, and disposal records.

## Category 5: Difficult inputs and edge-case testing

**41. School Management System**  
Build a school management system for a school with 1,200 students. Include attendance, fee tracking, examination results, parent communication, and teacher access. The budget, delivery date, and technology stack have not been decided.

**42. Urgent Website Redesign**  
Redesign the company's website within two weeks. The CEO wants a modern interface, mobile responsiveness, improved accessibility, online payments, a customer portal, and integration with three legacy systems. The development team has only two members.

**43. Project With Conflicting Stakeholders**  
Implement a new employee performance management system. HR wants extensive evaluation forms, employees want minimal paperwork, managers want customizable reports, and IT requires strict data access controls. The project sponsor has not resolved these competing priorities.

**44. Incomplete Project Description**  
Create a mobile application for a small business to improve customer engagement. The business has not specified its target audience, budget, timeline, required features, or success metrics.

**45. Unrealistic Cost Reduction Target**  
Reduce total operating expenses by 50% within one month without reducing headcount, service quality, or production capacity. Analyze the feasibility of the request and identify the information required before committing to the target.

**46. High-Risk Data Migration**  
Migrate ten years of customer and transaction data from a legacy database to a new platform. Data quality is inconsistent, documentation is incomplete, and the business cannot tolerate data loss. Develop a migration, validation, rollback, and contingency approach.

**47. Low-Budget Startup Launch**  
Launch a food delivery startup in one city within ₹2 lakh and eight weeks. The founders require a customer app, delivery partner app, restaurant portal, online payments, order tracking, and basic analytics.

**48. Failed Project Recovery**  
Recover a delayed ERP implementation that is four months behind schedule and 25% over budget. Several integrations are incomplete, employee training has not started, and the original project manager has left. Prepare a recovery charter and governance approach.

**49. Vague AI Implementation Request**  
Use artificial intelligence to improve productivity across a 200-person organization. No business problem, use case, budget, implementation timeline, data availability, or success criteria have been defined.

**50. Conflicting Deadline and Scope**  
Deliver a new inventory management platform within 30 days. The requirements include barcode scanning, supplier integration, demand forecasting, role-based access, mobile support, audit logs, and executive dashboards. The project has a fixed budget and limited engineering capacity.
